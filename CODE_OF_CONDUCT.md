# Code of conduct

BloomCode is a place to learn. People arrive at every level, and asking a basic question is part of learning.

## Expected

- Be patient and kind, in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding someone meant harm.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, including about anyone's background, identity or experience level.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, and publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, and repeated off-topic posts after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules. To report something privately, contact the organization owners through GitHub. Reports are handled in confidence.
